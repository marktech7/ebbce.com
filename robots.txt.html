ABOUT US

E.B. Balisacan Consulting Engineers, is a professional, dynamic, and progressive organization engaged in the structural design, investigation, and value engineering of residential and commercial, from low to high rise buildings and industrial buildings.


Behind the growth of the firm from its humble beginning, is the able leadership of our Principal/Executive Engineer, Engr. Elmer B. Balisacan, who topped the Civil Engineering Board Examination in November 1984. Engr. Balisacan’s “vision” for an excellent service and an understanding of needs of our client, caused him to put up a structural engineering consultancy firm that will meet the needs of clients with excellent yet competitive professional fees.


Having been employed with top architectural and structural engineering firms, notably, G & W Architects, Chiyoda Corporation, and G.E. Origenes & Associates, and once partner of the Pimentel & Balisacan International, Engr. Balisacan was able to make use to full advantage his vast experience in structural engineering and construction management.


The firm is well versed not only in local design codes but also with international standards as well, since we have done projects in other countries such as Japan, Thailand, Guam, Saipan, Indonesia, Vietnam, Nigeria, Angola and the Middle East.


The company is prepared to meet the challenges of the progressive globalization since our staff have extensive professional experience here and abroad and have worked in other reputable international construction and consulting companies.
